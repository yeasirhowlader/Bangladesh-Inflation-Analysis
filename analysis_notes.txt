BANGLADESH INFLATION ANALYSIS — ANALYSIS NOTES

Data Source:
World Bank — World Development Indicators

Indicator:
Inflation, consumer prices (annual %)

Data Period:
2010–2025

Key Findings:

1. The average inflation rate during 2010–2025 was 7.30%.

2. The highest inflation rate was 11.40% in 2011.

3. The lowest inflation rate was 5.51% in 2016.

4. Inflation increased significantly during 2022–2024.

5. Inflation reached 10.47% in 2024.

6. Inflation decreased to 8.77% in 2025.

Economic Interpretation:

Bangladesh's inflation rate fluctuated during 2010–2025. After reaching a high level in 2011, inflation declined during several following years. However, inflation increased significantly during 2022–2024 before declining in 2025.

The data show that consumer price inflation varied considerably across the period.

Important Note:

The data represent annual consumer price inflation measured using the Consumer Price Index (CPI). Inflation rate should not be confused with GDP growth or the overall price level.

Limitations:

- The analysis uses annual data.
- Monthly changes are not included.
- Inflation rates alone do not explain all changes in the economy.
